FAQ

Public feed videos, Reels, photos and carousels, and IGTV. Original quality, no watermark added.

No. This tool only works with public content and never asks for your Instagram password.

Can I download private posts, Stories or Highlights?

No. Private content, Stories and Highlights require an account login, which this tool deliberately does not do.
